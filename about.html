About

A compassionate, culturally responsive approach to counselling and coaching.

Welcome

Thank you for being here. Taking the step to look for support takes courage, and I am glad you found your way to this page.

Amber Coaching & Counselling Services was created around a simple belief: every story deserves understanding, compassion and respect. Everyone arrives with their own history, culture, and way of seeing the world, and good support begins by honouring that.

I work with individuals, couples, and families who are facing life's challenges, whether that is grief, relationship strain, parenting worries, or the feeling of being stuck and wanting something to change.

What guides my work

  • Compassion. You will be met with kindness, never judgement.
  • Respect. Your values, culture, and choices matter.
  • Confidentiality. What you share stays private, within the limits explained in advance.
  • Collaboration. We set goals together, and you lead the way.

My approach

Sessions are conversational and shaped around what you need. Some people want a place to be heard. Others want practical tools, new perspectives, or help setting goals. Often it is a mix, and that is perfectly fine.

I offer culturally responsive and multilingual services, because feeling understood is easier when you can speak openly in your own words, from your own background.

Good to know: Counselling and coaching can be very helpful, but they are not a substitute for medical care, psychiatric treatment, or emergency services. If you need those, I will gladly help you think about where to turn.
