Fees

Clear, fair pricing on a sliding scale, so cost does not stand in the way of support.

A sliding scale, so support is within reach

I believe cost should not stand in the way of getting support. Fees for 60-minute sessions are offered on a sliding scale, and the fee that fits your situation is agreed together, in advance, in a private and respectful conversation. You will never be asked to prove anything.

SessionFee
60-minute session, standard fee$145
60-minute session, sliding scale (reduced fee, agreed in advance)from $100
90-minute session$120

Payment

  • Payment is due at the time of each session, unless other arrangements are made in advance.
  • Fees may be reviewed with prior notice.
  • Your fee is confirmed before we begin, and is written in your service agreement.

Cancellations

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • Late cancellations or missed appointments may be charged the full fee, or a portion depending on the notice given.

Not sure which fee applies to you? Mention it on the service request form or call 587-707-7916. It is an easy conversation to have, and there is no pressure.

Questions about fees?

Ask any time. I am happy to talk it through before you decide anything.
