How It Works

Clear information so you know exactly what to expect.

Getting started

  1. Reach out

    Send a message or call. Tell me a little about what you are looking for. A few words is plenty.

  2. Have a first conversation

    We talk about what brings you here and what you hope for, and you can ask any questions. You decide if it feels right.

  3. Review the paperwork

    Before we begin you will receive a short service agreement, a confidentiality and privacy agreement, and a risk acknowledgement, so everything is clear and in writing.

  4. Begin your sessions

    Sessions are usually 50 to 60 minutes. We work at your pace, and you can pause or stop at any time.

Fees are on a sliding scale so that support is within reach. You will find the details on the Fees page.

Appointments & cancellations

  • Sessions are typically 50 to 60 minutes, unless otherwise agreed.
  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • Late cancellations or missed appointments may be charged the full fee, or a portion depending on notice.

Your privacy

  • Your information, notes, and records are kept confidential.
  • Records are stored securely, following Alberta's privacy laws and professional standards.
  • I contact you only through the methods we agree on.
  • Limits to confidentiality are explained in advance, in the confidentiality agreement.

Common questions

Is what I say really confidential?

Yes. Everything you share, including notes and records, is kept private and secure. There are a few limited exceptions, such as a risk of serious harm to yourself or others, suspected abuse of a child or vulnerable adult, or when the law requires disclosure. I will always try to talk with you first whenever possible.

How long is a session?

Sessions are usually 50 to 60 minutes. A longer 90-minute session is also available if you would like more time.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations or missed appointments may be charged the full fee, or a portion of it depending on how much notice was given.

Do I need to know what I want to talk about?

Not at all. Many people begin with just a feeling that something needs to change. We figure out the rest together.

Can I end counselling whenever I want?

Yes. You have the right to ask questions, make informed decisions about your care, and refuse or end services at any time.

Is counselling a replacement for medical or emergency care?

No. Counselling and coaching can support your well-being, but they are not a substitute for medical care, psychiatric treatment, or emergency services.

Ready to take the first step?

Reaching out is often the hardest part. Send a message or give a call, and we can talk about how I can support you.
