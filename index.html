How I can support you

Support for individuals, couples, and families, shaped around you and what you are going through.

Individual Counselling

A private, supportive space to talk through stress, life changes, relationships, self-esteem, or whatever feels heavy right now.

Couples Counselling

Support for partners who want to communicate more clearly, rebuild trust, and understand each other better.

Family Counselling

Help for families working through conflict, transitions, or communication so that home can feel calmer and more connected.

Grief & Loss Support

Gentle company through the loss of a loved one or any major loss, at whatever pace feels right for you.

Parenting Support

Practical, compassionate guidance for the challenges of raising children and teens, without judgement.

Life & Wellness Coaching

Goal-focused coaching to help you get clear on what matters, build healthy habits, and move forward with confidence.

What to expect

Safe, respectful, and at your pace

Your first step is simply a conversation. We talk about what brings you here and what you hope for, and you decide whether it feels like a good fit. There is no pressure and no judgement.

See how it works
  • A confidential space where your story is respected
  • Care that honours your culture, values, and language
  • Clear information about fees and policies before you begin
  • Your records kept private and secure, in line with Alberta privacy laws
  • You stay in charge: you can ask questions or end services at any time
